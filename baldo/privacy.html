Baldo Privacy Policy

Last updated June 26, 2026

Baldo is designed to be private. This policy explains how data is handled in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Baldo does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any developer server. The app has no account system and no developer-operated analytics or advertising trackers.

Photos and AI balding analysis

When you scan, Baldo analyzes the photo on your device using Apple frameworks. Your photos and scan results are not uploaded to us. The image analysis is local to your device.

Scan history

If you save scans, Baldo stores the scan image and result locally on your device so you can review your history. This history is not sent to us. You can delete saved scans in the app or remove all local data by deleting Baldo from your device.

Sharing

Photos and results leave your device only if you choose to share them using iOS sharing features, such as Messages or Save Image. Those actions are initiated by you.

Subscriptions

Baldo Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment information. Subscription management, billing, and refunds are handled by Apple.

Not medical advice

Baldo is a grooming and style tool. It is not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a treatment recommendation for hair loss.

Children

Baldo is a general-audience grooming tool and is not directed at children.
