Managing your subscription

Baldo Pro is billed through Apple. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple Account settings: open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → Baldo.

Your photos

Photos are analyzed on your device and are not uploaded. Deleting the app removes your saved scans. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
