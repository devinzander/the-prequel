Privacy Policy

Effective July 10, 2026

Callback is an interview practice app. This policy describes what happens to your data when you use it.

What we collect

Practice answers. When you record a practice answer, your speech is transcribed on your device using Apple's speech recognition. When AI coaching is available, the text transcript of your answer (never the audio) is sent to our server, which passes it to an AI provider (Anthropic) to generate your scores and feedback. Transcripts are processed to generate your feedback and are not used to train AI models.

Audio. Recordings are processed for live transcription and are not saved as audio files, on your device or anywhere else.

Scores and history. Your practice history, scores, and transcripts are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app, or using "Delete All Practice Data" in Settings, removes them.

What we don't collect

No account is required. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or location. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
