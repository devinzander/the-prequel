Privacy Policy

Effective July 11, 2026

What stays on your device

Your party plan, checklist, guest list, RSVPs, and registry drafts are stored locally on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app, or using "Delete All Data" in Settings, removes them from the device.

Publishing a registry

If you choose to publish a registry, the content needed to show the registry page is uploaded to our server: the retiree's name, company, years of service, party date, venue, and your registry items. The page lives at an unguessable link that you control and share. Republishing overwrites the page. To have a published page removed, contact us at the email below and include the link.

AI toast writer

When you use the toast writer, the details you type (names, stories, tone) are sent to our server, which passes them to an AI provider (Anthropic) to draft the toast. Requests are processed transiently, are not stored by us, and are not used to train AI models.

What we don't collect

No account is required. We do not collect your contacts (guests you add stay on your device), your location, or usage analytics. We do not use advertising SDKs.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.
