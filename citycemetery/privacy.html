Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

What the app does

City Cemetery is a free grave finder for the cemeteries inside Salt Lake City. When you search a name or open a map, the app requests records directly from the public registers that hold them: Salt Lake City's cemetery map server (maps.slc.gov), Mount Olivet Cemetery's public map layer (hosted on ArcGIS Online), the Utah Cemeteries & Burials Database run by the Utah State Historic Preservation Office, and the VA Nationwide Gravesite Locator. Those services see the name you searched and your device's network address, the same as a web browser would. Their own privacy practices apply to those requests.

Location

If you allow it, the app uses your location to show where you are on the cemetery map and to point you toward a grave. Location is used only while the app is open, is processed on your device, and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app works without it.

What stays on your device

Saved graves, recent searches, and your map preferences are stored only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What we don't collect

No account is required. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no purchases, and does not collect your contacts or usage data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.

About the records

Burial records are public records published by the cemeteries and the State of Utah. The app displays them as received and does not add to, edit, or store them beyond what you choose to save. Requests to correct a record go to the cemetery that keeps it.
