City Cemetery Support

City Cemetery finds a grave in any of the fourteen cemeteries inside Salt Lake City. Type a name, see the grave outlined on satellite, and walk to it.

Which cemeteries are mapped?

Salt Lake City Cemetery and Mount Olivet Cemetery publish a map of every grave, so the app can outline the grave and walk you to it. Mount Calvary, Elysian Burial Gardens, Fort Douglas Post Cemetery, the Brigham Young and Kimball-Whitney family cemeteries report to public registers, so the app can show the record and the plot text. The private cemeteries (B'nai Israel, Montefiore, Shaare Tzedek, Larkin Sunset Lawn, Wasatch Lawn, City View, St. Mark's) keep their own books; the app lists them with directions and a link to Find a Grave.

I can't find someone

Try the last name alone, then add a first name. Registers file names last-name-first and often use an older spelling, initials, or a married name. If the cemetery is one of the private ones above, the office is the only source.

A record is wrong

The app shows the register exactly as the cemetery or the state publishes it. Corrections go to the cemetery office; the app will show the change once the register does.

The grave outline doesn't match the stone

Survey data and GPS both carry a few feet of error. Use the plat, block, lot, and grave numbers on the record to confirm against the markers around you.
