The app

City Cemetery is a free iOS app that searches the public burial registers of the cemeteries inside Salt Lake City and shows mapped graves on a map. It is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.

The records

Every name, date, and plot shown in the app comes from a public register kept by a cemetery, Salt Lake City, the State of Utah, or the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. The app reads them live and cannot vouch for their accuracy; registers contain transcription errors, older spellings, and entries that lag the office's ledger. Confirm anything that matters with the cemetery office before you act on it.

Maps and directions

Grave outlines and walking guidance are drawn from the cemeteries' survey data and your device's GPS, both of which carry error of several feet. Watch where you walk, stay on paths where the cemetery asks you to, and follow each cemetery's posted rules and hours.

Acceptable use

Use the app for finding and remembering graves. Don't use it to harvest records in bulk, to overload the registers it reads from, or in any way that breaks the law or a cemetery's rules.
