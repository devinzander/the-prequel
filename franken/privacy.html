Franken Privacy Policy

Last updated July 11, 2026

Franken is designed to be private. This policy explains how data is handled in the app.

What we collect

No personal data. Franken has no account system and no developer-operated analytics or advertising trackers. We do not store your photos, scan results, or identity on any developer server.

Label photos and AI analysis

When you scan, the photo of the ingredient label is sent over an encrypted connection to our server, where an AI provider (Anthropic) reads the label to produce your result. Photos are used only to generate your result: we do not store them, and no account or identity is attached to them. Our AI provider retains request data for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring and then deletes it; your photos are not used to train AI models. Label photos are product packaging, not photos of you — avoid including people in your scan photos.

Scan history

If you save scans, Franken stores the label image and result locally on your device so you can review your history. This history is not sent to us. You can delete saved scans in the app or remove all local data by deleting Franken from your device.

Not medical or dietary advice

Franken describes food processing using the NOVA classification framework and plain-English explanations of label ingredients. It is informational only — not medical advice, a diagnosis, allergy guidance, or a substitute for professional dietary advice. Always read the physical label yourself for allergen and safety information.

Subscriptions

Franken Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment information. Subscription management, billing, and refunds are handled by Apple.

Children

Franken is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children.
