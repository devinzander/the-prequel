Privacy Policy

Effective October 15, 2026

What the app does

Old Utah is a free reader for three public sources on Utah's past. When you browse, search, or open something, the app requests it directly from the source's servers, the same way a web browser would, and that source's own privacy practices apply:

  • The Utah digital collections of the J. Willard Marriott Library at the University of Utah (collections.lib.utah.edu).
  • UtahRails.net and Don Strack's photo albums on SmugMug.
  • Cemetery records from Salt Lake City, Mount Olivet Cemetery, the Utah Division of State History's burials database, and the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs gravesite locator. A name you search is sent to these services to look it up.

What stays on your device

Saved items, recent searches, reading history, and your view preferences are stored only on your device. If you allow location access, it is used on the device to show where you are on a cemetery map and how far you are from a grave; it is never sent anywhere. We never see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What we don't collect

No account is required. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no purchases, and does not collect your location, contacts, or usage data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
