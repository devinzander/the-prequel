The app

Old Utah is a free, independent iOS app for reading the Utah digital collections published by the J. Willard Marriott Library at the University of Utah, Don Strack's UtahRails.net and photo albums, and the public burial registers of Salt Lake City's cemeteries. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the University of Utah, any contributing institution, any cemetery, Salt Lake City, the State of Utah, or the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

The content

Library items are served by the library and belong to the library or the contributing institution. Rights vary by item; each record lists its rights statement. Railroad pages, documents, and photographs belong to Don Strack and are presented with his permission. Burial records come from the cemeteries and agencies that publish them; corrections go to the cemetery. You are responsible for honoring the rights of any item you download, share, or reuse.

Availability

The app depends on its sources' websites. If a source changes or takes down its site, features of the app may stop working. The app is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
