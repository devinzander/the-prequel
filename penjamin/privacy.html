Penjamin — Privacy Policy

Last updated June 23, 2026

Penjamin is built to keep your information private. The app helps you track quitting THC, entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Everything you enter — your quit date, weekly spending, reasons, cravings, and streak — is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party.

No tracking, no ads

Penjamin contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not build a profile on you and we do not sell or share any data.

Notifications

Milestone and check-in reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent through any server.

Subscriptions

Penjamin Plus is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information.

Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all of it.
