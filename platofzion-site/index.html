What it shows

The public record, made legible.

Utah publishes an enormous amount about every parcel — it's just scattered across county assessors, recorders, and state GIS services. Plat of Zion pulls it together into one tap.

01 STATEWIDE

Every county

Parcel boundaries for all 29 Utah counties, compiled from the Utah Geospatial Resource Center.

02 DEED

Owner of record

Who holds the parcel, wherever the county publishes it — Salt Lake and Utah counties today, more as they open their data.

03 ASSESSOR

Acreage & value

Deeded area and assessor market value, set in a clean serif ledger — the numbers that actually matter, up front.

04 ZONING

Zoning & districts

Zoning class plus the civic districts a lot sits in: legislative, school board, and voting precinct.

05 CIVICS

Who represents it

The current legislators and officials tied to each district, matched from a live public roster.

06 PRIVATE

Private by design

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your location is used on-device to center the map and never leaves your phone.
