Privacy Policy

Effective July 17, 2026

Plat of Zion has no user accounts, no ads, and no third-party tracking. The app collects a small amount of anonymous, non-identifying information described below. It is never linked to you and never used to track you across other apps or websites.

Location

If you tap the locate button, your device's location is used on-device to center the map on the parcel you're standing on. It is never transmitted, stored, or shared.

Parcel and civic data

To show you information about a parcel, the app requests public records directly from government and public open-data services — including the Utah Geospatial Resource Center, county assessor and recorder services, municipal (city) GIS services for zoning and city-council districts, and state services for legislative, school-board, and voting-precinct districts. It also downloads a public roster of current legislators. These requests include only the parcel or map area you are viewing — never your identity or your device's location. Each service is operated independently by its government or public provider and is subject to that provider's own terms.

Saved parcels

Parcels you bookmark are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app.

Anonymous usage

When you first open the app, an optional survey asks what brings you to Plat of Zion and what you're looking for. If you answer, your selections — together with your device type (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) — are sent anonymously to help decide what to build next. This contains no personal information, is not linked to your identity, and is not used for advertising or tracking. You can skip it.

Launch email (website only)

If you enter your email on platofzion.com to be notified at launch, it is stored solely to send that one announcement and is never shared or used for any other purpose. This applies to the website; the app itself does not collect your email.

Contact

Questions? Email info@platofzion.com.
