Snack Stocker — Privacy Policy

Last updated July 6, 2026

Snack Stocker is an arcade game about keeping a vending machine stocked. This policy explains how data is handled in the app.

What we collect

Nothing, directly. Snack Stocker has no account system and no developer-operated servers or analytics. Your scores, unlocked skins, and settings are stored only on your device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

Snack Stocker shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising partner. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as an advertising identifier (IDFA, where you allow it), IP address and approximate region, and how you interact with ads. This collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy; details on how Google uses this data are at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether Snack Stocker may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to personalize ads. If you decline, you'll still see ads — they'll just be less personalized. You can change this anytime in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In some regions you may also see a consent form from Google before ads load.

Remove Ads

The Remove Ads purchase permanently turns off banner and interstitial advertising. Rewarded ads (like continuing a run) remain available but only ever load when you choose to watch one.

Game Center

Leaderboards are optional and provided by Apple's Game Center under Apple's Privacy Policy. We never receive your Game Center identity or friends list.

Purchases

Remove Ads and machine skins are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children

Snack Stocker is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
