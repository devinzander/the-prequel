Snowball Privacy Policy

Last updated July 11, 2026

Snowball is designed to be private. This policy explains how data is handled in the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Snowball has no account system and no developer-operated analytics or advertising trackers. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal or financial data on any server.

Everything stays on your phone

Your debts, balances, rates, and payoff plan are stored only on your device. Snowball makes no network requests with your financial data — there is no server to send it to. Delete the app and the data is gone.

Not financial advice

Snowball computes payoff projections from the numbers you enter, using standard snowball/avalanche methods with fixed-rate assumptions. It is informational only — not financial, credit, or investment advice. For decisions about your finances, consult a qualified professional.

Subscriptions

Snowball Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through your App Store account. We do not receive or store your payment information. Subscription management, billing, and refunds are handled by Apple.

Children

Snowball is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children.
