Purchases

Remove Ads is billed through Apple. If your purchase doesn't appear, tap Restore Purchases on the About & Photo Credits screen. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Ads

The Remove Ads purchase permanently disables all advertising — interstitials, the results-screen banner, and rewarded hint ads (hints become free). See our Privacy Policy.
