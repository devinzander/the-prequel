Tailwater — Privacy Policy

Last updated July 13, 2026

Tailwater shows live river conditions and fish-stocking reports. It is built to know as little about you as possible.

What stays on your device

Your followed rivers, flow ranges, and preferences are stored on your device. Your location, if you allow it, is used only to center the map and pick your state — it is never sent to us or anyone else.

What our server receives

If you turn on stocking or flow alerts, your device's anonymous push token and your list of followed waters are stored on our server so we can send those notifications. This token identifies your device to Apple's push service only — it is not linked to your name, email, or any account, and we don't have accounts. Turning alerts off, unfollowing, or deleting the app orphans this data, and it is purged.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics

Tailwater contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not build a profile on you and we do not sell or share any data.

Data sources

River readings come from the U.S. Geological Survey; stocking reports come from state wildlife agencies. Requests to fetch this public data go directly from your device or through our server without any personal information attached.

Subscriptions

Tailwater Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive or store your payment information.
