Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

What the app does

The Data Center is a free reader for the Utah digital collections published by the J. Willard Marriott Library at the University of Utah (collections.lib.utah.edu). When you browse, search, or open a record, the app requests that page, image, document, or recording directly from the library's servers, the same way a web browser would. The library's own privacy practices apply to those requests; see lib.utah.edu.

What stays on your device

Saved items, recent searches, and your view preferences are stored only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app removes them.

What we don't collect

No account is required. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no purchases, and does not collect your location, contacts, or usage data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
