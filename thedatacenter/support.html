About the archive

The Data Center reads the Utah digital collections published by the J. Willard Marriott Library at the University of Utah: photographs, manuscripts, newspapers, oral histories, and films from twenty Utah institutions. The library hosts everything; the app is a phone-first way to browse, search, and keep it. Questions about a specific item, its rights, or a correction belong with the library: Ask Us.

Price

The Data Center is free. No account, no ads, no purchases.
