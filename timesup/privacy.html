Privacy Policy

Times Up: Retirement Countdown — effective July 11, 2026

The short version: Times Up collects nothing.

What stays on your device

Your retirement date, work schedule, and settings are stored only on your device (and in the app's private widget storage on that same device). They are never uploaded anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party SDKs.

Purchases

The one-time Times Up Plus unlock is processed entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Purchases restore through your Apple ID.

Notifications

If you turn on milestone alerts, notifications are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent from a server.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
