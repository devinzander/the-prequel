Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

What the app does

Utah Rails is a free reader for UtahRails.net, Don Strack's history of railroading in Utah, and for his photo albums on SmugMug (donstrack.smugmug.com). When you browse, search, or open a page, document, or photo, the app requests it directly from utahrails.net or SmugMug, the same way a web browser would. Those sites' own privacy practices apply to those requests; see utahrails.net and SmugMug.

What stays on your device

Saved pages, reading history, recent searches, and your view preferences are stored only on your device. We never see them. Deleting the app removes them.

Photos

If you choose to save a photo, the app asks for add-only access to your photo library. It never reads your photos.

What we don't collect

No account is required. The app has no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no purchases, and does not collect your location, contacts, or usage data. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
