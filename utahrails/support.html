About the site

Utah Rails reads UtahRails.net, Don Strack's in-depth history of railroading in Utah: the railroads, the mines and industries they served, and the locomotives and cars that ran here, along with his photo albums on SmugMug. The site hosts everything; the app is a phone-first way to browse, search, and keep it. Questions about the content itself belong with the site: Contact UtahRails.net.
